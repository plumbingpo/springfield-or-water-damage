Signs You Need Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Springfield

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Gallons keep spilling from a ruptured water heater tank.
  • Water is coming through a ceiling, light fixture or electrical outlet.
  • Several hours or more have passed with hardwood, drywall or carpet sitting wet.
  • Water from a floor above is streaming down interior walls.
  • Trapped water overhead has made part of the ceiling bulge or sag.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.
  • What is on the floor came up from the sewer, or it is discolored and smells foul.

How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Know where your electrical panel is so power to wet rooms can be cut safely.
  • Wrap plumbing in cold, unheated parts of the house so a hard freeze cannot crack it.
  • Run a check on the sump pump ahead of wet weather, and think about adding battery backup.
  • In basements and other low areas, keep documents and valuables up on shelves.
  • Swap worn hoses feeding the washer, sinks and toilets rather than waiting for a rupture.
  • Place leak sensors near the water heater, washing machine, dishwasher and under sinks.
