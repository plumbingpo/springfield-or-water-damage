When to Call for Storm Damage Repair in Springfield

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Ceiling drywall that sags or bulges, or paint that bubbles after rain
  • Flooding in lower levels because drainage systems were overwhelmed
  • Gutters or downspouts torn loose, bent or dumping water against the house
  • Water pooling in the attic, around the chimney or near skylights
  • Metal flashing pulled away from vents, valleys or the edge of the roof
  • Water stains that show up on ceilings or walls during or after a storm
  • Rooms that start to smell stale or moldy following a storm

Storm Damage Repair Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Install impact-resistant windows or storm shutters if you live in a hurricane-prone area
  • Take dated photos of the house every year, since insurers ask about prior condition
  • Learn where the main water shutoff is and how to close it if pipes are damaged in a storm
  • Keep gutters and downspouts clear so water drains properly in heavy rain
  • Before storm season each year, get the roof inspected and its problem areas repaired
  • Extend downspouts and slope the soil so runoff moves away from the foundation
